CGPA Calculator — All Semesters, Any Scale

CGPA, or Cumulative Grade Point Average, is the overall average of all your Semester GPA results earned throughout your degree. Unlike a term result, Cumulative GPA reflects your complete academic journey from the first semester to your most recent, combining grades and credits into one number shown on transcripts and degree certificates.

Understanding GPA vs CGPA is essential. A Semester GPA measures performance in a single term, such as Fall or Spring, while CGPA represents the average across all completed terms. To convert term results into an overall standing, our tool helps you calculate GPA to CGPA accurately by weighting each semester by its credit hours rather than treating every term equally.

Universities use CGPA because it provides a consistent, standardized way to evaluate long-term consistency. It is used for degree classification, honors and Dean's list eligibility, academic probation, and graduation eligibility. Because it averages all semesters, it rewards sustained effort and improvement over time.

To Calculate CGPA, most schools use a weighted average: each Semester GPA is multiplied by its semester credits, those products are summed, and divided by total credits. If credits are equal, you can use the simple version — CGPA = Sum of Semester Grade Points ÷ Number of Semesters — to quickly estimate your average. Try our GPA Calculator for single terms first.

Your CGPA matters for scholarships, university admissions, internships, graduate school applications, and employment where employers set minimum CGPA cutoffs. Maintaining a competitive cumulative average opens doors to merit-based aid and advanced programs, which is why regularly tracking your Cumulative GPA throughout your degree is so important.

Quick Answer

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is your overall GPA calculated across all semesters combined. It is computed by dividing your total quality points earned across all terms by your total credit hours attempted across all terms.

How CGPA Is Calculated

Your Cumulative Grade Point Average is the weighted mean of all your semester results. Most universities calculate CGPA by multiplying each Semester GPA by its credit hours, summing those products, and dividing by total credits attempted.

CGPA = Σ(Semester GPA × Semester Credits) / Σ(Total Credits)

When every semester carries the same number of credits, the calculation can be simplified to:

CGPA = Sum of Semester Grade Points ÷ Number of Semesters

In weighted calculations, a semester with 18 credits influences your final CGPA more than a semester with 12 credits. This ensures that heavier academic loads are fairly represented. Example Calculation

Let's walk through a sample calculation for a student with four completed semesters:

Semester Semester GPA Credits Weighted Points (GPA × Credits)
Semester 1 3.20 15 48.0
Semester 2 3.50 16 56.0
Semester 3 3.80 15 57.0
Semester 4 3.60 18 64.8
Totals 64 225.8

The calculation in plain English: (3.20 × 15) + (3.50 × 16) + (3.80 × 15) + (3.60 × 18) = 48 + 56 + 57 + 64.8 = 225.8. Divide that by total credits (64) to get a CGPA of 3.53. Frequently Asked Questions

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is the weighted average of all your Semester GPAs across your entire degree program and represents your overall academic performance from your first semester to your most recent one.

How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is calculated using a weighted average: multiply each Semester GPA by its credit hours, add all those weighted values together, and divide by the total credit hours. If each semester has the same credits, you can use the simple formula CGPA = Sum of Semester GPAs ÷ Number of Semesters.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA usually refers to your grade average in a single semester or term, while CGPA stands for Cumulative GPA and is the average of all your semester GPAs combined across your whole academic program.

Can I calculate CGPA from GPA?

Yes. To calculate CGPA from GPA, combine all your Semester GPAs along with their respective credit hours. Our CGPA Calculator does this GPA to CGPA conversion automatically. If you need to calculate a single term first, use the GPA Calculator and then add it here.

Is this calculator accurate?

Yes. The calculator uses the standard weighted CGPA formula used by most colleges and universities. It accounts for different credit loads per semester to give you a precise cumulative average.

Is this CGPA Calculator free to use?

Absolutely. The Scholarics CGPA Calculator Online is completely free, requires no signup, no account, and works instantly. Your data is saved locally in your browser for convenience.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The CGPA Calculator is fully responsive and optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktops. You can calculate CGPA on any device without installing an app.

Does every university calculate CGPA the same way?

Most universities use the weighted credit formula, but some institutions have variations, such as using unweighted averages or different grade point scales. Always check your university handbook for the exact method, but this calculator covers the most common standard.
