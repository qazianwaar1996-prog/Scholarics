GPA Calculator — Weighted & 4.0 Scale

Grade Point Average (GPA) is a numerical representation of academic achievement that averages the final grades earned across courses, most commonly using a 4.0 scale. An A typically equals 4.0 points, a B equals 3.0, and an F equals 0.0. This metric helps standardize performance evaluation across different courses and instructors.

Your GPA matters because it is widely used by colleges for admissions, by employers during hiring, and for determining eligibility for scholarships, honors programs, and academic probation status. Maintaining a competitive GPA can significantly impact your future educational and career opportunities.

Semester GPA measures your performance during one specific academic term, whereas cumulative GPA (CGPA) reflects your overall average from all semesters completed to date. Many students use semester results to monitor progress toward their cumulative goal. For a full picture of your academic history, explore our CGPA Calculator.

Weighted GPA calculation multiplies the grade points of each course by the number of credit hours assigned to it. These weighted values are added together and divided by the total credits attempted. This ensures courses worth more credits influence the final average more than lower-credit ones.

How GPA weighting works

Your GPA is calculated by multiplying the points for each grade by the credit hours for that course. These are summed up and divided by the total number of credit hours.

GPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credits) / Σ(Total Credits)

Example GPA Calculation

Let's walk through a sample calculation with three courses:

Course Grade Credits Grade Points Weighted Points
English 101 A 3 4.0 12.0
Calculus I B 4 3.0 12.0
World History A- 3 3.7 11.1
Totals 35.1

The calculation: (4.0 × 3) + (3.0 × 4) + (3.7 × 3) = 12 + 12 + 11.1 = 35.1. Divide by total credits (10) to get a GPA of 3.51. This demonstrates how credit weighting affects the outcome in plain English.

What is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It is a numerical measure of your academic performance calculated by averaging the grade points earned in your courses.

How is GPA calculated?

GPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade points by its credit hours, summing these values, and dividing by the total credit hours.

What is weighted GPA?

Weighted GPA assigns more importance to courses with higher credit hours. Courses with more credits have a greater impact on your overall GPA.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA usually refers to your performance in a single semester, while CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the average across all semesters in your academic program.
