Attendance Percentage Formula

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100

Does attendance affect my grade?

At most institutions, attendance below the minimum threshold results in not being allowed to sit exams, regardless of academic performance. Some courses also award marks for participation.

What counts as a class?

Lectures, tutorials, labs, and practicals usually all count separately. Check your institution's policy on what constitutes an "attended" class.
