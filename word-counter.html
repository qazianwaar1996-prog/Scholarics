Why track word count?

Most academic assignments come with strict length requirements. This tool allows you to monitor your progress in real-time, helping you hit your targets without exceeding word limits. Reading time is estimated at a standard academic pace of 200 words per minute.

Is my writing private?

Yes. Your text never leaves your computer. We use local browser storage to save your draft, meaning only you can see what you write.

Does it count symbols?

Characters include every letter, space, and symbol. The "No spaces" metric ignores whitespace to give you the raw content count.

Why word count matters in academic writing

Most academic assignments specify a word count range — not as an arbitrary constraint, but as a calibration of depth. A 1,500-word essay requires substantive argument without padding; a 3,000-word report demands evidence, analysis, and nuance. Going significantly over or under the target usually signals either insufficient development or unfocused writing, and both affect grades.

This counter gives you an instant live count as you type or paste — tracking words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, and paragraphs so you can calibrate length in real time.

Does word count include references and footnotes?

It depends on the assignment instructions and institution. Many universities explicitly state whether references, footnotes, appendices, titles, and headings are included in the word count. The default assumption varies — some count everything, some exclude bibliography. If the instructions are unclear, email your instructor or check your department's style guide before submitting.

This counter includes all text you paste or type, including headings and any reference list. Exclude what you need to exclude by not pasting those sections, or subtract manually.

What counts as a word?

In standard word count, a word is any sequence of characters separated by spaces. Hyphenated words (well-being) are typically counted as one word. Numbers written as digits (2026) count as one word. Contractions (don't) count as one word. Most word processors and this counter follow the same convention, so counts should match within 1–2 words of Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

How do I reduce word count without losing meaning?

The most effective cuts: remove redundant qualifiers ("very", "quite", "really", "in order to"), cut passive voice in favour of active, eliminate throat-clearing intros ("It is important to note that..."), and merge sentences that make the same point. Aim to cut 10–15% on first pass — most academic writing is improved by tighter expression.

How many words per minute does the average student read?

Average adult reading speed is 200–250 words per minute for comprehension. Academic reading (dense text requiring careful processing) is typically 100–150 wpm. A 3,000-word paper takes roughly 20 minutes to read carefully — useful to know when estimating how long your marker will spend with your submission.

Can I use this for checking application essay limits?

Yes. Paste your essay and compare against the stated limit. The Common App personal statement has a 650-word hard cap. Supplemental essays vary from 150 to 650 words. Many university portal systems automatically reject submissions over the limit, so checking before pasting into the application form is strongly recommended.
