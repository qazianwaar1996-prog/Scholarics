Pakistan Grading System: CGPA and the HEC Scale

How the HEC-standard 4.0 CGPA scale works in Pakistan, how percentage maps to grade points, and how to convert Pakistani grades for study abroad applications.

Pakistan's university grading system

Pakistani universities accredited by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) predominantly use a credit-hour based semester system with a 4.0 CGPA scale — directly compatible with the US system for the purpose of international comparisons. This makes Pakistan one of the few countries where GPA conversion to US standards is largely 1-to-1, though some variation exists between institutions.

The standard HEC-aligned grading table:

A−80–84%3.7Excellent
B+75–79%3.3Very Good
B71–74%3.0Good
B−68–70%2.7Good
C+64–67%2.3Average

Some universities, particularly private institutions, may map 85%+ to A and set their own grade boundaries. Always verify against your institution's official grade policy. Use the CGPA Calculator to calculate your cumulative GPA across semesters on the 4.0 scale.

Percentage boundaries differ from the US

One critical difference between the Pakistani and US grading systems: the percentage ranges attached to letter grades are shifted upward in Pakistan. In the US, 93%+ is typically required for an A. In Pakistan, 85%+ earns an A at most HEC-accredited universities. This means a Pakistani student with 80% has an A−, while the same score would be a B+ or B in the US.

This matters greatly for international applications. A CGPA of 3.5 from a Pakistani university reflects strong performance against different percentage standards. When submitting applications to US, UK, or Australian universities, include your transcripts showing the actual percentage marks alongside the CGPA — most international admissions offices are aware of the grade boundary difference.

Semester vs. annual examination system

Pakistan's higher education moved substantially toward the semester system following HEC reforms in the 2000s. However, many older public universities and affiliated colleges — particularly in Punjab and Sindh — still operate under the annual examination system, where students sit a single exam per year rather than at the end of each semester.

Under the annual system, results are expressed as a percentage of total marks rather than a CGPA. For postgraduate applications, students from annual-system universities often need to convert their percentage to CGPA. A common approximation used by Pakistani universities: divide percentage by 25 to get approximate CGPA on a 4.0 scale (so 75% ≈ 3.0). This is a rough conversion — use the Percentage to GPA Calculator for more precise results.

CGPA requirements at Pakistani universities

Graduation and dean's list requirements vary by institution, but common benchmarks are:

CGPA Typical standing
3.5 – 4.0Distinction / Dean's List
3.0 – 3.49High Merit
2.5 – 2.99Merit
2.0 – 2.49Pass
Below 2.0Academic warning / probation

Minimum graduation CGPA at most HEC-accredited universities is 2.0. Many competitive graduate programs require 2.5 minimum for admission, and 3.0+ for scholarship consideration.

Converting Pakistani CGPA for international applications

Because Pakistan uses a 4.0 scale, conversion to US GPA is broadly direct — a 3.5 CGPA translates to approximately a 3.5 US GPA. However, international admissions offices are aware that grade boundaries differ, and some may normalise Pakistani grades before comparison.

For UK applications, a Pakistani CGPA of 3.5+ (4.0 scale) is broadly equivalent to a UK Upper Second (2:1). For Australian applications, most universities accept WES or other credential evaluators. Use the Global GPA Converter for direct comparisons across 30+ country systems.

Attendance requirements in Pakistan

Most Pakistani universities mandate 75% minimum attendance as a condition for sitting final exams — a rule enforced through HEC's quality assurance framework. Falling below 75% typically results in being barred from the final examination and an automatic F for the course. Track your attendance in each subject with the Attendance Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CGPA in Pakistan?

On the 4.0 scale used by HEC-accredited universities, a CGPA of 3.5 and above is considered excellent (distinction). 3.0–3.49 is very good. 2.5–2.99 is satisfactory. For competitive postgraduate admissions and government jobs, a 3.0+ CGPA is typically expected.

Is 3.0 CGPA good for jobs in Pakistan?

A 3.0 CGPA meets most graduate-level hiring filters in Pakistan's corporate sector. Many multinationals and top Pakistani companies set 3.0 as a minimum cutoff for campus recruitment. For finance, consulting, or civil service entry, 3.5+ is more competitive. Technical roles in engineering and IT are often less GPA-focused, weighting skills assessments more heavily.

How do I convert my percentage marks from Intermediate or A-Levels to CGPA?

Intermediate (FA/FSc) results are in percentage form. A rough conversion to 4.0 CGPA: percentage ÷ 25. So 75% ≈ 3.0, 80% ≈ 3.2. For A-Levels: A* ≈ 4.0, A ≈ 3.7, B ≈ 3.3, C ≈ 2.7, D ≈ 2.0, E ≈ 1.3. These are approximate; institutions handle conversion differently.

Can I raise a low CGPA in my final year at a Pakistani university?

Yes, but the math gets harder with more credits on record. Use the GPA Improvement Planner to see exactly what semester CGPA you need to hit a target cumulative GPA. Also check if your university has a grade improvement policy that allows you to retake courses for grade replacement.
